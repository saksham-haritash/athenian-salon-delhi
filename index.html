Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Do I need to book an appointment in advance?

We recommend booking at least a day in advance for regular services and 2–3 days ahead for bridal packages or weekend slots. Walk-ins are welcome subject to availability.

What products do you use?

We use 100% premium international products from trusted global brands. Our stylists can recommend the right products based on your hair and skin type during consultation.

Can I customise a package?

Absolutely. Call us at 80111 10064 and we'll instantly customise a package for you — whether it's two services or a full makeover.

What are your working hours?

We are open every day from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM. We recommend calling ahead on public holidays to confirm timings.

Do you offer services for men?

Yes, we are a unisex salon. Our men's grooming services include haircuts, beard styling, facials, hair colour, and more.
